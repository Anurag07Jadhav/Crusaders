Club Heritage

Building Auckland’s Premier Cricket Culture

Founded in 2023, Crusaders Cricket Club was established with one clear vision—to build a highly competitive cricket team capable of challenging for championships while creating a culture of commitment, discipline, and continuous improvement.

Based in Auckland and proudly affiliated with Eden Roskill Cricket Club, Crusaders welcomes players who are committed to improving, competing, and representing the club with pride. We back committed players regardless of prior experience.
